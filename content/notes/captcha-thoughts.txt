2026-07-15 — Opinion

I've been thinking about the CAPTCHA recommendation I made for the university portal. It's the right short-term fix, but I want to be clear: CAPTCHA is not a security silver bullet.

What CAPTCHA stops:
- Unsophisticated automated scraping
- Script-kiddie level attacks
- Bulk enumeration without proxy rotation

What CAPTCHA does NOT stop:
- Credential stuffing with CAPTCHA-solving services
- Manual attacks by determined adversaries
- Attacks that bypass the UI entirely (API abuse)

The real defense is layered: rate limiting + account lockout + strong passwords + MFA + audit logging + CAPTCHA. Each layer catches what the others miss.

Don't let a CAPTCHA give you false confidence.
